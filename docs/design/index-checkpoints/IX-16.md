## What was built

One React tree at every width, as before: on a phone `.ix-frame[data-layout="phone"]` makes the sidebar and main two
screens, and everything below gives them their phone shape. The layout rules are `styles/ix-phone.css` (imported last);
the moves are Web Animations, transform and opacity only.

### What a phone is (`components/ix/shell/phone/phoneLayout.ts`)

`useIsPhone()`: under 768px wide (`useMobile()`), **or** a touch screen under 500px tall (`(max-height: 499px) and
(pointer: coarse) and (hover: none)`), so an iPhone held sideways (844x390) stays a phone instead of getting the
desktop's two columns. `useIxLayout`, `SheetFrame`, `CommandPalette`, `IxMenuSurface` and `ui/ContextMenu` all ask it,
and AppShell's phone branch now reads `useIxLayout()` (it used Glass's `useGlassLayout`). `usePhoneLandscape()` is the
sideways half alone (the call sheet uses it).

### The root (the sidebar)

- Full width. The **dock is a bottom bar**: 64px plus `--safe-bottom`, on `--ix-side-end`, its top hairline, 44px
  targets around the 34px marks (`--ix-dock-item: 44px`), the indicator under the current place, sideways scroll as
  IX-6 built it. No name tooltips (no hover on a finger).
- **Your face at the header's right** (`PhoneAccountButton`, 44px target, opens the account menu), "…" left of it, both
  in the server header (IX-5's `SidebarHeader`) and People's header (IX-14's `PeopleSidebar`). On a phone People's header
  drops its left face (it would be your face twice) and keeps its words. The toolbar's avatar is hidden on a phone.
- Held sideways both screens keep clear of the notch (`--safe-left` / `--safe-right` padding on the screens, toolbar and
  strip).

### Pushed pages (`glass/shell/phoneMotion.ts`, `usePhoneFrame.ts`, `phoneNav.ts`; IX-16 owns them now)

- One push model for every phone push: a **top** page, the screen **under** it, and a **shade** (a dim layer inside the
  under screen, `.ix-phone-shade`, `color-mix(--ix-scrim 50%)`), all placed by one number `x` (0 pushed, 1 gone):
  top `translateX(x·100%)`, under `translateX(-30%·(1-x))`, shade opacity `1-x`.
- Main pushed over the sidebar: **360ms on `IX_SPRING`**, the root (and its dock) moves **-30%** and dims beneath it
  (it was -22% with a fade). Ids `ix-phone:top|under|shade`. Reduced motion: a 150ms crossfade in place. Nothing fills
  forwards; the resting states are CSS.
- **52px toolbar** under the status bar; its folded fill reaches up under the status bar like a navigation bar. Back
  chevron, folded title, search, members; 44px tools. `--ix-toolbar-h` is 52 on a phone (92 with the call strip), so
  every page that lays itself out under the toolbar (they all read the variable) follows.
- **30/700 large title** (`.ix-large-title__h`), folding as on desktop.
- Column padding 16; the **message box** 8px from the edges and above the home indicator.
- A **tap on the row that is already open** pushes main again (`usePhonePush` in `IxFrame` now keys on the location's
  `key` and only on `PUSH` navigations: a redirect replaces, the browser's back pops; neither pushes).

### The back gesture (`phone/useEdgeSwipe.ts`; replaces `hooks/useSwipeGesture.ts`, deleted with its test)

A finger from the left 24px that moves right **drags the page with it**: the screen beneath follows from -30% and
brightens. Let go past half way (or flick right faster than 0.45 px/ms) and it finishes from where it was let go
(duration scaled by the distance left, at least 140ms); otherwise it springs back. What it drags is decided when the
finger lands:

1. a pushed sheet over the screen in front (it is closed by its own back button, the sheet marked `data-ix-dismissed` so
   its CSS leave does not replay, and `skipNextSheetPop()` so the covered screen's return does not either);
2. main over the sidebar (`showScreenSettled('places')`: the frame does not replay the move);
3. a page the route pushed (a thread: the toolbar's `onBack`), or a page pushed inside a sheet: those cannot follow a
   finger (the page before them is not mounted), so a swipe of 60px or a flick runs their back.

A field, `[data-no-swipe]`, a sideways scroller, a dialog or a menu keeps the edge; a drag that turns vertical is a
scroll. AppShell turns it off under the call and under settings only.

### The call strip (`phone/CallStrip.tsx`, mounted by `IxFrame` in main on a phone)

40px under the toolbar on every pushed page while you hold a call (`heldCard`): the amber speaker (phone glyph for a
direct call, podium for a stage), the channel, up to three faces, the waveform while someone talks, and your microphone
(join-mute preference while listening; none for a stage audience). Tapping it opens the call sheet. Somebody calling you
uses the same strip: "Maya is calling", **Decline** and **Accept**. It slides down from under the toolbar when a call
starts on an open page, the page following it (`ix-call-strip:in|page`, 360ms). Test ids `ix-call-strip`
(`data-state="yours|incoming"`), `ix-call-strip-mic`, `ix-call-strip-decline`, `ix-call-strip-accept`.

`hooks/useIncomingDmCall.ts`: declines are now **shared** (a tiny store) so a decline on the strip also silences the
root's pinned card; previously each mounted copy kept its own list. `forgetDeclinedCalls()` resets it (session reset and
tests).

### The call as a sheet (`components/ix/call/CallSheet.tsx`)

`CallSlot` renders the unchanged `CallView` inside `CallSheet` on a phone:

- Top inset `--safe-top + 8`, radius 24 on the top corners, a grab handle, a scrim; controls above the safe area.
- **Rises out of the strip** (on a pushed page) **or the card** (on the root), 420ms on the spring, with the content in
  a counter-layer (`counterKeyframes` from `callMotion.ts`); from the bottom edge when neither is on screen
  (`ix-call-sheet:open|open-content|scrim`).
- **Drag down** by the header or the handle: it follows the finger (a sixth of any upward pull); past a third of its
  height, or a downward flick over 0.5 px/ms, it goes on down off the screen (`ix-call-sheet:dismiss`, 360ms) and the
  view closes like Back to chat (the call goes on; every video unsubscribed, §7.3); otherwise it springs back
  (`ix-call-sheet:return`). The drag holds the native-video guard (`beginCallMove`) like the other moves.
  **Back to chat**, Leave and Esc shrink a still copy back into the strip or card
  (`ix-call:close`, IX-13's `playClose`).
- **One column** of people for one or two, **two** above that; with cameras the tiles keep 16:9 at the column's width.
- A **stream sits 16:9 at the top**, everyone else below it in the same two columns, scrolling.
- **Full screen**: the header's **Full screen** control (IX-13's `CallHeader` shows it on a phone when a stream is in
  focus; it is the stage's theater mode) or **a phone held sideways with a stream in focus**: the stream alone,
  letterboxed (whole width upright, whole height sideways), with **Back to chat** and (upright) **Exit full screen**
  floating at the foot.
- No chat column on a phone (IX-13's rule, kept).

### Sheets are pushed pages (`glass/sheets/SheetFrame.tsx` + `phone/pushedSheets.ts`)

On a phone a sheet is the whole screen on `--ix-main`, slides in from the right (360ms spring) and out the same way; the
screen it covers moves -30% and dims beneath it (`<html data-ix-pushed>` holds that resting state; `ix-phone-sheet:*`
plays it). Its header leads with a **back chevron** that closes it (`data-ix-sheet-dismiss`), not the close X; a page
pushed on a sheet keeps its own back plus the close. A sheet replaced by another (people then search) is no pop and no
push: the close waits one frame. The exit hold is the push's 360ms on a phone.

### Menus are action sheets (`IxMenuSurface`, `ui/ContextMenu`, `phone/actionSheet.ts`)

On a phone every Index menu (server, channel, People, account, message, profile, Send later) and the shared context menu
(a row held down, the room card) is a **bottom action sheet**: 8px from the sides above the safe area, the rows in one
radius-20 group (rows 52, 16px, glyphs 20), a separate **Cancel** below, over a scrim; it rises 360ms on the spring and
drops 240ms (`ix-action-in|out`); reduced motion fades. Keyboard shortcut hints are not shown on a sheet
(`.ix-menu__hint--key`). The call's own popovers (microphone, More, share quality) sit at the foot the same way
(CSS over their inline coordinates).

`ix/sidebar/useLongPress.ts`: after a press that opened a menu the finger's lift cancels the browser's compatibility
mouse events, which otherwise land on the new scrim and close the menu at once.

### Quick switcher

The whole screen from the sidebar's search field (IX-9 already), now with a **Cancel** in the field instead of the Esc
key cap, 16px input (no zoom on focus), and a short rise.

### The message box and the keyboard (`phone/usePhoneRoot.ts`)

`<html data-ix-layout="phone">` for the portaled surfaces, and the on-screen keyboard from the visual viewport: while it
is up main is laid out to what is visible (`top: --ix-vv-top`, `bottom: --ix-keyboard`), the box sits on the keyboard
(its home-indicator margin dropped), the root behind is hidden, and a conversation read to (near) its end stays at its
end. Where the browser resizes the layout itself (Android `resizes-content`) the inset is 0 and nothing changes.

### Smaller fixes

- The **pull to refresh** indicator (MessageList, IX-11's "pull lamp") is an Index ring under the toolbar
  (`.ix-pull`), shows nothing inside its dead zone (a tap or a sideways swipe used to light it), and its fade no longer
  stays in `document.getAnimations()` after it ends.
- A one-to-one DM on a phone gets **"…" (Conversation options)** in the toolbar, opening its channel menu (Catch up,
  Pinned messages, Media, Copy link) as an action sheet: the door IX-15 flagged (no right click on glass; its title is
  the profile). Desktop is unchanged.

## Decisions and deviations

1. **Landscape is a phone.** §9 says "< 768px"; the plan's check is "390x844 and 844x390". A short touch screen counts
   (see above); a short desktop window does not.
2. **The rise is a grow** out of the strip or card (a FLIP with a counter-layer, like the desktop's open), and a plain
   rise from the bottom only when neither is on screen. The close shrinks into the same place; a drag-dismiss slides
   down.
3. **No scrim tap to dismiss the call sheet**: the visible scrim is 8px under the status bar; the drag, Back to chat
   and Esc dismiss it (and a click handler on scenery fails the a11y audit).
4. **No shadow on the pushed edge**: a shadow on main would show at the screen's right edge while main is parked off
   it; the dim shade carries the depth.
5. **Action sheets keep Cancel** as a separate row (iOS's pattern); a tap on the scrim also dismisses.
6. **Full screen is the stage's theater mode** (`setStageMode('theater')`), so Esc/T and the frame's `data-stage` keep
   their meaning; on a phone the view itself still draws no theater chrome (`CallView`'s `theater` stays false there).
7. Account menu anchor on a phone: the sheet does not need one; `PhoneAccountButton` carries `data-ix-account-anchor`.
8. The plan card truncation IX-10 saw at 390: with the phone's 16px column padding the Events page's cards and the Plan a
   night draft fit with nothing ellipsized (asserted in the spec). The past event's "Completed" tag wraps under its
   title with its own left margin (IX-8/IX-10's styling, left as is).
