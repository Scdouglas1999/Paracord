# IX-18 checkpoint: Motion gate and polish

Contract: `docs/index-spec.md` §5 (every moment), §10 (the live region), §1.2 and §1.9 (quiet at rest; transform
and opacity only). Plan row: `docs/design/index-plan.md` §3 (IX-18) and §5.4.4. Worktree
`~/Documents/Paracord-index`, branch `feat/index-ui`. Ports 4663 / 4665.

The owner's bar for this package: "really slick animations around the app for different things, without being
overbearing and having everything moving all the time." Event-driven only; nothing animating at rest.

## What was built

### The motion gate (`client/e2e/motion-gate.spec.ts`, rewritten for Index)

The Glass gate (14 cases on the Glass scene, all failing since IX-4) is replaced. The new one runs on the IX-0
harness and holds every §5 moment to three things:

- **The moment.** Each animation that IS the moment runs exactly the spec's duration, on the spec's curve (spring
  `cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1)` / ease `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`), from the spec's start keyframe and opacity. The
  numbers are the spec's, cited by section at each assertion, never read back from the app.
- **The law.** Only `transform` and `opacity` move, plus colors (§5.13, §5.15). Anything else in any moment fails
  by name. Nothing loops but the talking waveform.
- **Quiet at rest.** Two seconds after each moment settles, `document.getAnimations()` is empty (the waveform aside
  while somebody talks; the talking-ring case proves even that goes to exactly zero when they stop). A moment that
  sets off a later one by design (the New divider closing two seconds after you read; "Link copied" turning back)
  settles too; a loop never does and fails.

Cases (22): §5.1 place switch both ways; §5.2 channel switch down and up (and no toolbar fill flash); §5.3 preview
roll, time, unread dot in and out (and no fill flash on a channel that only has gateway messages); §5.5 room goes
live, row in, row out (the sidebar under the card closes up once the row has gone), room empties (one FLIP), the
one-time ping for another server and its announcement; §5.4 the pinned card FLIP to and from its server, still
between other places; §5.6 Plan a night and §5.15 the action-card lift; §5.7 join (yours layer, the face's flight,
the name held to 45%, "You joined General"); §5.8/5.9 open (counter-layer at 0 until exactly 40%, whole by 75%),
theater both ways, back to chat; §5.10 an arrival and the make-room; §5.11 the send flight is not clamped (620ms,
travels > 40px); §5.12/5.13 the box opening in three slices, the feed FLIP, the send control's color, closing in
350ms; §5.14 the fold (scroll-linked: the large title never animates; folded title and toolbar fill transitions);
§5.15 dock and row hovers; §5.16 copy feedback there and back; §5.17 sheets in and out, menus in and out; §5.18 a
toast in and out; §5.19 the ring 150 in / 300 out and the waveform only while talking (and "Jeremy started sharing"
said); §5.20 category collapse; reduced motion (place, channel, card, no ping, no lift, join, open, close, arrival,
a sheet and a menu all 150ms opacity-only; the waveform still); and a **self-test**: the channel switch with an
injected `height` animation and a `box-shadow` transition fails the gate naming both, as does a drifted duration,
while the clean record passes.

The recorder is `client/e2e/fixtures/motionFixture.ts`: an init script that wraps `Element.prototype.animate` and
hears `transitionrun` / `animationstart`, so a moment's record is every animation it started (not a frame sample).
One-event steps the gate needs: `client/e2e/fixtures/index/steps/motion.ts` (`voiceJoin`, `voiceLeave`, `speak`,
`share`).

It runs both ways:

```
cd client
npm run test:motion                                    # playwright.config.ts, 4173/4175 (stub = app port + 2)
PARACORD_E2E_INDEX_SPEC=motion-gate PARACORD_E2E_INDEX_PORT=4663 PARACORD_E2E_INDEX_RT_PORT=4665 \
PARACORD_E2E_INDEX_WP=ix18 npx playwright test -c e2e/index-review.config.ts
PARACORD_E2E_MOTION_VERBOSE=1 …                         # prints every moment's record
```

The frame-budget sampling of the Glass gate is gone (it measured Glass stage costs on a GPU-less runner and was
flaky by its own admission); frame cost and WebKitGTK CPU are IX-20's (§5.4).

### The motion lint (`client/scripts/motion-lint.mjs`)

Now also enforces §1.2: an animation that repeats forever (CSS `infinite`, `iterations: Infinity`, Tailwind
`animate-pulse/-ping/-bounce`) needs a named allowance in `LOOPS` saying what event it lasts for. Ten are named
(the talking waveforms, typing dots, the speaking ring and writing breath on the design tokens page, the loading
screen mark, the upload sweep, a bot button's request in flight, a sports loading skeleton); `animate-spin` is a
spinner and passes. A stale allowance fails. The header now cites index-spec. No transform/opacity allowance added.

### Main's entrance (§5.1, §5.2): was missing

Switching channel played Glass's route crossfade (`contentIn`, 4px, 240ms) on the whole outlet; §5.2's title and
feed entrance did not exist. Now `components/ix/motion/mainEntrance.ts` + `useMainEntrance.ts` (mounted by
`IxFrame`): the large title from `translateY(dir×14+4)` 420ms and the feed from `translateY(dir×22)` 480ms, both
spring, `dir` from the sidebar rows (`[data-place-row]` hrefs in the pane that is not leaving; 0 for a jump), a
part that mounts late (the timeline replacing its skeleton) still enters within 600ms, a newer switch commits a
running one, nothing on a phone (the push is the move), 150ms fades under reduced motion. `AppShell`'s
`useContentSwap(location.pathname)` is removed.

**Decision:** a place switch enters with `dir` 0 (title rises 4px, feed only fades), as the prototype plays it
(`go()` calls `drawMain(true)`, no direction) while the sidebar slides; the spec said 1. Spec §5.1 updated.

### Live region (§10)

`components/ix/a11y/` (new): `IxAnnouncer` (one `role=status` polite region, mounted once by `IxFrame`, test id
`ix-announcer`), `announcer.ts` (paced: at most one announcement every 2s, a burst said together, same words twice

### Item 4: moment by moment against the prototype

Filmed both at 0/60/120/200/320/480ms with every animation paused and seeked (`ix18/film/strip-*.png`,
`sbs-*.png`): place switch and channel switch match frame for frame (after the entrance above). Tuned:

1. **Room goes live** (`strip-arrive-side.png` before, `arrive-app-strip3.png` after): the translucent card faded
   in over the rows still sliding down beneath it (text through the card at 60-120ms). The prototype does not push
   at all. Now the card enters 120ms behind the push it causes (`CARD_IN_DELAY_MS`, `fill: backwards`), when the
   spring has moved the rows two thirds of the way, and the card slot sits above the rows (`z-index: 2`). Spec §5.5
   updated.
2. **Room empties** pushed the sidebar twice (34px then 142px): the slot compared against a stale height, because a
   leaving row is removed by `PeopleRows` without a render of the slot, so the content under the card **jumped**
   34px when a person left. `CardSlot.usePushBelow` now also follows the slot with a ResizeObserver: after a row's
   250ms exit the sidebar FLIPs up 450ms (§5.5 "then rows below FLIP up"), and an emptying room FLIPs once.
3. **Join**: the prototype shows the new controls at once while your face flies; ours held them at 0 until 45%,
   leaving an empty card for ~340ms. The hold is now on your name only. Spec §5.7 updated.
4. **Open call**: the prototype's tiles are drawn throughout the grow; ours faded from 40% to 100%, so the view was
   half-empty when the surface arrived. The counter-layer now holds at 0 until exactly 40% (it began at the 37.5%
   sample) and is whole by 75% (`CALL_CONTENT_TO`). Spec §5.8 updated.
5. **Menus left without their leave** (§5.17 "out 120ms"): `MenuHost` unmounted a menu the moment its slot
   cleared. It now keeps the closed menu rendered ~450ms (`useKeptMenu`) and passes `open`, so `IxMenu` plays
   `ix-pop-out` (and a phone's action sheet its own drop).
6. **Toasts** left in 150ms; §5.18 says 200 (`Toast.tsx` `LEAVE_MS`, `pc-toast-out`).
7. **The toolbar's folded fill flashed** on switching to a channel whose history had not said where it starts (the
   title is not mounted for a commit or two): `useTitleFold` folds for a missing title only when the list overflows
   and the title stays away 160ms (`MISSING_TITLE_MS`).
8. **Reduced motion**: the sheets', menus', switcher's, profile card's and the sidebar dot's 150ms fades were dead
   (the app-wide switch zeroes every animation with `!important`); they now carry `!important` and play.
9. **Sports**: the live dot and the drive bar pulsed for as long as a game was live; still now (§1.2), the score
   flash on a change stays.

Matches without change (numbers in the gate, frames read): selection glide, preview roll, dock indicator glide and
