## What was built

`IxConversation` (`components/ix/conversation/`) replaces the Glass `ConversationPane`. GuildPage, TextChannelView
and DMPage render it; the Glass header (the duplicate search / members / avatar tools IX-4 noted), `ConversationPane`,
`useChannelMenu` and `useDmCall` are gone.

```
.ix-conv[data-subject]                      the host: height 100%, --ix-hue, --ix-timeline-top = the toolbar's 58
  MessageList  header=<LargeTitle + notices>  emptyState=<EmptyConversation | LockedConversation>  onScroller
    .ix-timeline__scroller > .ix-timeline__header  .ix-tl-col.ix-conv-head > LargeTitle (+ pinned notices)
  .ix-composer-column                          the box's column (IX-12's host class)
    JoinerCard?                                first visit, populated server (see Decisions 5)
    MessageInput
  DropTarget                                   a portal into .ix-main
```

## Decisions and deviations (read these)

1. **s0-plan-a-night differs from the reference in two places, both deliberate.** (a) The draft sits on the message box
   (bottom 714), 58px lower than the reference's 656, because the spec's feed is bottom-anchored (IX-11 decision 2: the
   prototype's `min-height: 540` leaves 58px under it). It lands where the real message will, so Post it does not move it.
   (b) The sidebar still says "No messages yet" until the event is posted: the prototype writes "You planned Game night ·
   Fri 9 PM" at the click, but an unposted draft is not a thing the server (or the sidebar's preview rule, §4.5) knows.
   After Post it the preview is the event post, "Game night · Fri 9 PM" (no "You planned"), by IX-5's rule.
2. **Banners span the window, not only main.** §6.8 says "across the top of main above the toolbar (the existing
   `BannerStack` and `--pc-banner-inset`)". That mechanism pads the whole shell (`AppShell`'s `paddingTop`), so the sidebar
   moves down too (shot `conversation/banner-restart.png`). Putting it over main alone needs a host in `IxFrame` and a
   toolbar offset (IX-4's files); not done.
3. **The pinned score and a stage session scroll with the title.** `ChannelAmbient` and `StageChannelPanel` are under the
   large title as the spec and IX-10 say, which is the `header` slot, which `MessageList` shows only once the start of the
   channel is loaded. In a long history the pin is above the loaded messages until you scroll to the top. If a pinned
   score must always be visible, it needs a non-scrolling slot (MessageList's or the frame's).
4. **Banner placement of the update notice.** It was a bottom-right card with Download / Dismiss and "View release notes".
   It is a strip now (one text action, a dismiss); "Release notes" stays as a link in the text.
5. **The joiner welcome has two faces.** In an empty channel it is the welcome (cards: Say hello, See who's here, Pick
   roles when the server's onboarding has roles and you have not finished it). On a first visit to a server that already has
   messages it is a compact card above the message box (same actions, dismiss with ×), because the large title (and
   anything under it) is not mounted while older history is unloaded. "Joiner" = not the server's owner and not yet
   welcomed (the old `guild-welcomed:<id>` flag, so nobody is welcomed twice). The flag is read once per visit; the full
   welcome marks it when it is shown, the card when it is dismissed or used. "Pick roles" brings the onboarding page back
   (`GuildPage` re-mounts the gate with a new key).
6. **DM title is the profile card (spec), so pins / media / summary have no visible door in a one-to-one DM.** Right click
   (or the menu key) on the title opens the channel menu. A visible door (the profile card's "..." or a toolbar item) is a
   follow-up for IX-9 / IX-16; flagged so it is chosen, not found.
7. **The invite dialog is IX-9's.** "A second press opens the invite sheet" is `openInvite` (IX-9's dialog).
   That dialog makes its own 7-day invite when it opens, so a second press leaves two invites on the server.
8. **The welcome centering.** The block is centered in what is left under the title with 58px taken off the bottom: the
   prototype's feed ends 58px above the box, so s0's mark is at y=242 (reference 241), the cards end at 561 (reference 561).
   The line-height is the prototype's `normal` (the app's 1.5 made the card 150px, not 146).
9. **Stacked cards are rows.** Under 640px of column the three cards stack (spec); each lays out as tile-beside-words so
   three fit above the box at 900x700.
10. **No welcome crossfade for another person's first message.** The send flight ghosts the empty state out for yours; for
    someone else's first message `MessageList` swaps the empty state out at once (it owns that render) and the arrival motion
    pops the new row.

## For IX-18: the send flight's ghost crossing the bubble above (read, not changed)

IX-11 warned that with groups 3px apart the translucent ghost crosses the bubble above at about 110ms. In the filmed frames of the
busy channel (`ix15-all/send/busy-general/110ms-crop.png`, `150ms-crop.png`): at 110ms the half-filled grey ghost ("count me in, give
me ten minutes") sits with its top edge 11px into the bottom of "yep, bringing the projector" and its words are still the quiet ink;
at 150ms it is cream and its top-left 40x8px overlaps that bubble's bottom-right corner. It lasts roughly 90 to 150ms (two or three
frames at 60fps). It does not hide any text of the bubble above (the overlap is its bottom padding), and in motion it reads as the
new message passing in front of the last one. Still, a grey half-fill over a grey bubble is the least clear moment of the flight.
If it reads badly in your motion pass, the options that do not touch the constants: draw the ghost layer under the row it crosses
only while it is translucent (z-order), or give the landing row's `margin-top` a few px more for your own rows only. I did not change
either. The empty-channel flight has nothing above it and is clean.
