## Decisions and deviations

1. **The reply line sits above the bubble** (§4.11), so `sendFlight.ts`'s `replyChip` now also finds the chip in
   the row (`[id^="msg-"]`) and, when it is outside the bubble, clones it without the bubble's inks. Additive;
   new unit test; the in-bubble case is unchanged. The ghost's fill is `--ix-cream` with no shadow (Index
   bubbles have none) so the 620 ms frame lands as the exact bubble.
2. **Short conversations sit on the message box** (spec §3.1 "bottom-anchored"). The prototype's s2/s4/s6 leave
   58 px under the feed (its `.feed{min-height:540px}` ignores the scroll's 58 px top padding); the spec's
   behavior wins, so those feed crops are 58 px lower than the reference. s5 (overflowing) matches row for row.
3. **No "Today 9:58 PM" divider in s5**: the 30-minute rule (IX-0's data note 2). "Today 9:41 PM" now opens every
   conversation (the prototype's; Glass opened bare).
4. **Spacing is the prototype's**: 9 px above a group, 3 inside, and your own message 3 px under whatever is
   above it (`.mine` has no margin). This puts your landing row closer to the row above than Glass did, so at
   110 ms the translucent ghost crosses the previous bubble during the fill window. The flight itself is
   unchanged.
5. **"Mark unread" is not offered**: the server's read cursor only moves forward (`read_states.rs`), so it
   cannot be honored. Needs a server change if wanted. Save for later, Copy message ID, Report, Reveal author and
   Bulk delete stay in the menu (existing features).
6. **The capsule sits at top −22 px** (over the bubble's top padding, never the words); on a short bubble it
   covers the name line while hovered, and on a reply it covers part of the quoted line while hovered.
7. **New divider fades 2 s after the reader is at the bottom** — opening a channel lands at the bottom, so it is
   seen for 2 s on open; then it fades (300 ms) and the rows close up (450 ms, composite add).
8. **Skeleton**: three static bubbles stand at the top of the list whenever older history exists (`hasMore`),
   instead of appearing when the fetch starts, so nothing jumps when a page is asked for.
9. **Compact face size** comes from the `message_display_compact` setting (the source of `data-density`); the
   rest of compact density is CSS on `:root[data-density='compact']`.
10. `EphemeralMessage`'s note stays inside the bubble (IX-8 suggested under it; that needs its API changed).
11. The pull lamp (phone pull-to-refresh) is left as it was: IX-16's.
